A situation you have probably seen
An owner gets an offer, it is a good offer, and it is only at that point that anyone works out what they will actually live on afterwards.
Dana,
You will have watched this happen. The business is worth what everyone hoped. The proceeds land. And then somebody does the arithmetic on what that amount produces as income for the next thirty years, and the number is smaller than the owner assumed, usually by a lot, and by then every decision that could have changed it is already behind them.
I work the other end of that. When an owner is a few years out, the questions are still open: what they will need, what the business has to be worth to produce it, and what has to change in the meantime. It is unglamorous and it is mostly arithmetic, but it is a very different conversation at year three than at closing.
You see these clients long before I do. If one of them is starting to talk about getting out, I am happy to be a sounding board with no expectation attached, and no interest in touching anything you already handle for them.
