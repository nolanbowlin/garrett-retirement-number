The two conversations owners never have together
Dana,
Good speaking last week. You asked what I actually do for an owner client, so here is the short version in writing.
An owner heading for an exit gets two pieces of advice that never meet. The deal side works out what the business is worth. Nobody works out what the owner needs it to be worth. Those are different numbers, and the gap between them is the thing that decides whether somebody can retire on the proceeds or has to go back to work in a different form.
That gap is the whole of my work. It sits before the deal, not during it, which is why it does not collide with anything you do.
