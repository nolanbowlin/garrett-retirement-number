Two versions of everything

PREVIEW is the approval copy. Filled in, ready to put in front of Garrett. The figures in the report preview are the real output of the tool's own model for the household described in it, so it reconciles if anyone checks the arithmetic.

TEMPLATE is the working file, with {{tokens}} where the content goes. This is the one that gets wired into the sending platform.

Each preview is generated from the template beside it by build-previews.js. Change a layout in the template and re-run node build-previews.js to bring the previews back in step. Never hand edit a -preview.html, it gets overwritten.

Outreach

Correspondence

Five formats for cold and early contact with referral partners: CPAs, business and M&A attorneys, brokers, bankers. Not for clients, not for people who came in through the tool, and not for anyone already on the newsletter.

The counterintuitive one is the letter. It is the plainest template in the folder and it beats the prettier ones on a first touch, because the recipient is deciding one thing in two seconds: is this a person or is this a campaign. A masthead and a button answer that the wrong way. Sequence, partner types and the compliance limits are in correspondence/correspondence-notes.md.

The rules

What applies to every send

Compliance

The firm disclosure block is not editable. It is Garrett's registered disclosure, not boilerplate, and trimming it for design reasons is a compliance problem rather than a layout choice.

Nothing sends without a review ID. Every approved public piece carries a Guardian advertising review ID and expiry in the footer, in the format their own site uses, 8894350.1 Exp 5.2028. There is a {{review_id}} token waiting in each template.

Market commentary is the highest risk content in the suite. Descriptive, never predictive. No forecasts, no performance figures for a named holding, nothing that reads as a recommendation. If a send has nothing safe to say there, delete the block rather than reaching for something.

A physical address and a working unsubscribe link are required by CAN-SPAM on all of it.

Production

Never overwrite a published image. The asset host caches hard and mail already delivered cannot be repaired. Changing an image means a new filename, never an overwrite.

Watch the file size. Gmail clips a message over roughly 102KB and hides everything after the cut, including the unsubscribe link. The largest file in this suite is 28KB, so there is room, but images and long copy eat it quickly.

Full detail on all of this is in the brand package.
