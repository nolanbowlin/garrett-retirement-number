What this was built on

Every figure is in today's dollars, so nothing here has been inflated and nothing needs discounting back. Your invested assets are assumed to grow at 5 percent a year above inflation until you stop, which is deliberately below a pure stock market assumption because almost nobody within fifteen years of retiring is holding only stocks.
